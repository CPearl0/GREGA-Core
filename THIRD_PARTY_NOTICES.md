# Third-party assets

GREGA-Core's original code and original assets are MIT-licensed.

The following assets are derived from [GregTech CEu Modern, v7.0.2-1.21.1](https://github.com/GregTechCEu/GregTech-Modern/tree/v7.0.2-1.21.1), by the GregTech authors and contributors, under LGPL-3.0:

- `assets/grega_core/textures/item/laminated_nano_*.png` and the mainframe animation metadata: original nano circuit artwork with one extra stacked layer and purple chip accents, modified for GREGA.
- `assets/grega_core/textures/item/basic_electronic_component.png`: original basic electronic circuit artwork with half the pin groups removed, modified for GREGA.
- `assets/grega_core/models/block/machine/*_inscriber.json`, `*_steam_mixer.json`, the corresponding item models and blockstates: GT forming press, steam compressor and mixer model templates adapted to GREGA's namespace and machine identities.

These derived assets retain LGPL-3.0. See `licenses/GTCEu-LGPL-3.0.txt`. The editable PNGs and JSON sources are included in the GREGA-Core source repository. Original textures credit GregoriusT, Mr_Touchyou, Zerrens and the GT community, as acknowledged by GTCEu.
